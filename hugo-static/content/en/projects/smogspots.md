## The smog issue

Many European countries suffer from smog. There are various pollution sources and legislation could not solve the issue so far. In Eastern Europe, the main pollutants are the individual households which use fuels of low quality for heating. So-called low emission is hard to be eliminated, even though local governments support the replacement of old-styled furnaces. SmogSpots is a winning project of Smogathon 2019, created by "AI Embassy". We aim to detect the households which pollute the air and support the authorities to help the owners of these properties to solve their issues.
